03. Wallets & Funds

Deposits are tracked, tips stay internal, withdrawals are on-chain

Custodial vault architecture engineered for zero-gas community micro-tips.
  • HD & EC Derivation: Each user receives unique, permanent cryptographic deposit addresses for supported chains: USDT (Polygon PoS), SOL (Solana SPL), ETH (Ethereum), BTC (Bitcoin SegWit), and LTC (Litecoin).
  • Encryption at Rest: All generated custodial keys are protected under bank-grade authenticated Fernet AES-256 encryption at rest. Keys are never exposed in Discord channels or DMs.
  • Instant Social Tips: All tips executed via /tip or $tip are internal database ledger transfers settled in sub-50ms with zero blockchain gas fees.
  • On-Chain Withdrawals: External withdrawals via /withdraw are genuine blockchain transactions broadcasted to public nodes. Once broadcasted, blockchain transactions are irreversible.
  • Address Accuracy: You are strictly responsible for verifying the accuracy of external withdrawal addresses. We cannot recover funds sent to an incorrect address.
  • Zero-Loss Guarantee: Off-chain balances are never deducted if an on-chain transaction fails to broadcast or encounters an RPC network error.
