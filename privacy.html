BitForge ("we", "our", or "the Bot") values and respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, how it is safeguarded, and your rights regarding your data when using the BitForge Discord application.

By using BitForge, you consent to the collection and handling of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Data-Minimization Principle: BitForge strictly collects only the minimal data required to process cryptocurrency transactions and maintain accurate custodial ledger balances within Discord.

A. Discord Account Identifiers

  • Discord User ID: A unique numeric snowflake ID assigned by Discord (e.g., 1201968157464739920). This is required to associate your custodial balance, deposit addresses, and transaction records with your Discord account.
  • Discord Username: Used solely for visual rendering on receipts, profile embeds, and tipping confirmations.
  • Discord Server (Guild) ID: Collected only when configuring server-specific preferences (such as custom prefixes).

B. Blockchain & Financial Ledger Data

  • Public Wallet Addresses: Permanent public blockchain addresses generated for deposits across supported networks (Polygon, Solana, BSC, Ethereum, Bitcoin, Litecoin).
  • Encrypted Private Keys: High-entropy cryptographic private keys generated to manage custodial deposits, stored strictly under Fernet AES-256 authenticated symmetric encryption.
  • Off-Chain Balance Records: Internal ledger amounts representing your account credit for each supported currency.
  • Transaction History: Public on-chain transaction hashes, internal tip logs, amounts, timestamps, and recipient Discord User IDs necessary to maintain accurate accounting.

C. Data We DO NOT Collect

  • NO Real Names or Government IDs
  • NO Physical Addresses, Phone Numbers, or Emails
  • NO IP Addresses, Locations, or Browser Fingerprints
  • NO Credit Cards or Banking Credentials
  • NO Discord Message Content (BitForge processes only commands matching the prefix or slash command schema).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data exclusively for the operational functionality of BitForge:

  1. Wallet Generation: Deriving unique deposit addresses for your account.
  2. Transaction Execution: Processing incoming deposits, instant off-chain social tips, and external on-chain withdrawals.
  3. Ledger Integrity: Maintaining an immutable accounting record of balances and receipts.
  4. Security & Abuse Prevention: Guarding against double-spending, exploitation, and macro spam.
  5. Display & Command Outputs: Rendering profile stats, balance overviews, and transaction history.

We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or share your data with advertisers or third-party marketing brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security Standards

  • Cryptographic Encryption: All custodial private keys are encrypted at rest using AES-256 in CBC mode with HMAC SHA-256 authentication. Decryption occurs exclusively in volatile server memory when executing authorized transactions.
  • Database Isolation: The database utilizes SQLite with Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) and strict foreign key integrity constraints, protected by filesystem-level access restrictions.
  • Ephemeral Interaction Messaging: Sensitive outputs (such as address generation and balance details) utilize Discord ephemeral messages visible only to you.
  • No Plaintext Key Exporting: BitForge never reveals or outputs private keys into public channels or direct messages.

4. Third-Party Services & Integrations

BitForge interacts with the following external services to deliver bot features:

  • Discord API: To receive user interactions, slash commands, and deliver messages. Governed by the Discord Privacy Policy.
  • Public Blockchain RPC Nodes: Public nodes (Alchemy, Infura, Solana RPC, QuickNode, Blockstream, Blockchair) are queried to verify on-chain deposits and broadcast raw signed withdrawal transactions.
  • CoinGecko API: Read-only price oracle to fetch real-time USD exchange rates without transmitting user identifiers.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

  1. Retention Period: We retain your Discord ID and transaction ledger for as long as your account is active on BitForge to preserve accounting audit trails.
  2. Right to Access: You can inspect all data stored about your account at any time by running /balance and /history.
  3. Right to Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"):
    • You may request complete deletion of your account from BitForge at any time.
    • Before requesting deletion, you should withdraw remaining cryptocurrency balances via /withdraw.
    • Upon verified request, your user record, encrypted private keys, and pending settings will be permanently wiped from our active database. Historical transaction hashes remain permanently on the public blockchain where data is inherently immutable.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

BitForge is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain EEA jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has used the service, we will promptly delete their associated records.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material updates will be announced via the bot help menu or official support server. Your continued interaction with BitForge following modifications constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
